## Full post

Free STEM workshops for curious kids. Live today: https://builders.ascent-i-tech.in 🚀

At Ascent Intelligent Technologies we have been building something small and useful: Builders of Tomorrow, a free website where children aged 8 to 14 learn how engineers think by actually doing it.

Every workshop is built around the engineering design cycle. Kids ask what the problem is, imagine several answers, plan one, create it, test it and improve it, then go round again. The site walks them through each step with a checklist, and the simulations are where the loop happens: in the Water Rocket Lab they choose a bottle, fins and a nose cone, pump up the pressure and launch a 3-D rocket; change one thing, launch again, and the fair-test habit builds itself. In Bridge Builder they glue craft sticks into triangles and load the bridge until it creaks, with the forces coloured so they can see which piece is pulled and which is pushed.

The worksheets are what make the learning stick. Each workshop has them at three levels: Explorer (Rocket Detective: what makes it fly? Triangle Power), Builder (The Water Experiment, Pressure Power, The Strongest Shape Test) and Engineer (Fair Test and Variables, Budget Engineer, an Engineering Design Journal). They ask children to predict before they test, record what they measured, explain what happened and plan the next improvement. Fill them in on screen, where they save automatically, or print them for a class.

What makes it work for grown-ups:

• No ads, no cost, no account needed to play
• Worksheets for every workshop at three levels, so it fits a classroom or a kitchen table
• Simulations are calibrated against real-world numbers and show their assumptions, so older kids can go deeper into the maths
• Sign in with an email code (or Google) to save designs and worksheet answers; under-13s use a parent or teacher email

If you teach, parent or mentor a young builder, I would love you to try it and tell me what breaks or what you would like to see next. More workshops are on the way.
