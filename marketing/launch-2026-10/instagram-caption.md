🚀 Build it. Test it. Make it better.

Builders of Tomorrow is an online playground where kids aged 8 to 14 learn how engineers think by doing it. Every workshop follows the engineering design cycle: ask, imagine, plan, create, test, improve, then go round again. Kids design their own water rocket in 3-D, pump it up and launch it, then build a bridge out of craft sticks and load it until it creaks.

The learning sticks because of the worksheets. Each workshop has Explorer, Builder and Engineer levels that ask kids to predict, measure, explain and improve, from "Rocket Detective: what makes it fly?" to a full Engineering Design Journal. Fill them in on screen or print them for the classroom.
